## Pricing basis and scope

- Pi Hut costs are the supplier's current GBP product prices, including VAT,
  retrieved from its public product data for the exact linked variants. These
  are replacement prices and may differ from the amounts originally paid.
- Amazon kit costs are the builder's reported purchase prices. Each full kit is
  counted once, including spare pieces; the number of headers and standoffs
  actually fitted has not been itemised.
- One Nano board is used and was donated, so its cost to this project is £0.00.
  The builder reported an approximate retail price of €20 for the linked
  three-board pack (about €6.67 per board). That retail value is not charged to
  this build, so no currency conversion is needed for the project total.
- The total combines current Pi Hut prices, reported Amazon purchase prices
  and the donated Nano. It is a parts-cost estimate on that basis, rather than
  a verified total of historical spending or the cost to buy every item new.
- Delivery charges are excluded. Batteries, the page button, wiring, solder,
  tools and any enclosure are outside the supplied parts list and this total.
- Prices and stock availability can change; some linked products were out of
  stock when checked.
